# Body Weight Tracking - User Guide

## Quick Start

### Accessing the Feature
1. Log in to FitTrack Pro
2. Navigate to **Progress & Analytics** from the main menu
3. Scroll to the **Body Metrics** section

## How to Track Your Weight

### Adding Your First Entry

1. **Click "Add Entry"** button in the Body Metrics section
   - Or click "⚖️ Log Body Weight" in the Quick Actions section

2. **Fill in the required information:**
   - **Date**: Select the date of measurement (defaults to today)
   - **Weight (kg)**: Enter your weight (e.g., 75.5)

3. **Optional - Add more details:**
   - **Body Fat Percentage**: If you have a body composition scale
   - **Muscle Mass**: Your muscle mass in kg
   - **Measurements**:
     - Waist circumference
     - Chest circumference
     - Arms circumference
     - Legs circumference
   - **Notes**: Add context like "morning weight" or "after workout"

4. **Click "Add"** to save your entry

### Viewing Your Progress

Once you have entries, you'll see:

**Weight Summary Card:**
- **Current Weight**: Your most recent recorded weight
- **Change**: Total weight change from your first entry
  - Shows both kg and percentage
  - Green (📈) = weight increasing
  - Red (📉) = weight decreasing
  - Gray (➡️) = stable/no significant change
- **Trend**: Overall direction of your weight

**Entry List:**
- All your weight entries sorted by date (newest first)
- Each entry shows:
  - Date of measurement
  - Weight in kg
  - Body fat percentage (if recorded)
  - Edit and Delete buttons

### Editing an Entry

1. Find the entry you want to edit in the list
2. Click the **✏️ (edit)** icon
3. Modify any fields in the form
4. Click **"Update"** to save changes

### Deleting an Entry

1. Find the entry you want to delete
2. Click the **🗑️ (delete)** icon
3. Confirm the deletion when prompted
4. Entry will be permanently removed

## Tips for Best Results

### Consistency is Key
- **Weigh yourself at the same time each day** (preferably in the morning)
- **Use the same scale** for all measurements
- **Wear similar clothing** (or weigh yourself naked)

### Understanding Weight Fluctuations
- Daily weight can vary by 1-2 kg due to:
  - Water retention
  - Food in digestive system
  - Time of day
  - Recent exercise
- Focus on the **trend** over time, not daily changes

### Adding Context with Notes
Use the notes field to track factors that might affect your weight:
- "Morning weight, before breakfast"
- "After heavy workout"
- "Day after cheat meal"
- "End of week"

### Optional Measurements
While only weight is required, adding more measurements helps you:
- Track body composition changes
- See muscle gain vs. fat loss
- Monitor specific body areas
- Get a complete picture of your progress

## Understanding the Analytics

### Weight Change Calculation
- **Total Change**: Difference between your first and latest entry
  - Example: Started at 80 kg, now 75 kg = -5 kg

- **Percentage Change**: Relative change from starting weight
  - Example: -5 kg from 80 kg = -6.25%

### Trend Analysis
The system analyzes your last 5 entries to determine your trend:
- **Increasing (📈)**: Weight is generally going up
- **Decreasing (📉)**: Weight is generally going down
- **Stable (➡️)**: Weight is staying relatively constant

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I log multiple weights per day?
No, the system allows only one entry per date. If you need to update your weight for a day, edit the existing entry.

### What if I forget to log my weight?
You can backdate entries! Just select the appropriate date when adding a new entry.

### Can I see my weight history over time?
Yes! The Body Metrics section shows all your entries in chronological order. Future enhancements will include visual charts.

### What should I do if I enter the wrong weight?
Simply click the edit icon (✏️) next to the entry and update the weight value.

### Are my body metrics private?
Yes! Only you can see your body metrics. They are securely stored and protected by authentication.

### What units are supported?
Currently, the app uses:
- **Weight**: Kilograms (kg)
- **Body Fat**: Percentage (%)
- **Measurements**: Centimeters (cm)

### How often should I weigh myself?
For best results:
- **Daily**: If you understand weight fluctuations and focus on trends
- **Weekly**: A good middle ground (same day/time each week)
- **Bi-weekly/Monthly**: If daily fluctuations cause stress

## Troubleshooting

### "Failed to save body metrics"
- Check your internet connection
- Ensure weight is between 0 and 500 kg
- Verify date is not in the future
- Try logging out and back in

### Entry not showing after adding
- Refresh the page
- Check if the entry was actually saved by scrolling through the list
- Verify you're looking at the correct date range

### Can't edit or delete an entry
- Ensure you're logged in
- Check your internet connection
- Try refreshing the page

### Modal not opening
- Try refreshing the page
- Clear your browser cache
- Ensure JavaScript is enabled
